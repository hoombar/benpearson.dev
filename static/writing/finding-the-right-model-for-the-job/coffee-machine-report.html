Step-by-Step Replacement Guidelines

  1. Turn off the machine, unplug it from the wall, and remove the water tank.
  2. Open the front service door located behind the water tank.
  3. Squeeze the two red release buttons together and pull the brew group straight out.
  4. Pry off the two old red O-rings using your fingers or a blunt pick, taking care not to scratch the plastic grooves.
  5. Wash the brew group thoroughly with warm water and dry it completely.
  6. Apply a thin layer of food-grade silicone grease to the two new O-rings.
  7. Stretch and seat the new O-rings into the upper and lower grooves of the brew group.
  8. Slide the brew group back into the machine along the rails until the red buttons click securely into place.
  9. Close the service door, reinsert the water tank, and plug the machine back in.
