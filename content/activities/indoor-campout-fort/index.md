## What You'll Create

Turn a rainy afternoon into a wilderness adventure! You'll build a magnificent **Indoor Campout & Fort** using your sofa and a large **white sheet**. Then, you'll construct a safe, glowing indoor campfire from **cardboard tubes**, **tissue paper**, and a **flashlight** to tell stories around.

## Have fun!

- 👻 Take turns telling slightly-spooky or funny stories around the glowing fire.
- 🐻 Use your hands to make shadow puppets on the walls of the sheet tent.
- 🌭 Bring in some real snacks and pretend to roast hot dogs or marshmallows.
- 🌌 Lie on your backs inside the tent and stick glow-in-the-dark stars to the inside of the sheet.

## Why It's Amazing

* **Imaginative Play:** Transforms a familiar room into an entirely new world. 🏕️
* **Emotional Regulation:** Cozy, enclosed spaces help children feel safe and relaxed. 😌
* **Storytelling Skills:** Sitting around a 'fire' naturally encourages narrative building and sharing. 🗣️

## Pro Tips

For **ages 3-5**: Make sure the fort is tall enough for you to sit inside with them, as they might feel nervous in a totally enclosed dark space alone.

For **ages 5-8**: Let them engineer the fort structure themselves. Challenge them to incorporate multiple rooms or a 'tunnel' entrance!
