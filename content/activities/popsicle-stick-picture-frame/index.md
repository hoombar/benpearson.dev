## What You'll Create

Frame-worthy art deserves a frame-worthy FRAME! 🖼️ Your little artists will build a sturdy picture frame from **popsicle sticks**, paint it in gorgeous colours, decorate it with **stickers** and patterns, and use it to display their favourite drawing, painting, or family photo. It makes a brilliant homemade gift — especially for grandparents!

## Have fun!

- 👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 Make frames for the whole family — each person decorates their own style!
- 🌊 Theme your frame — seashells for a beach photo, leaves for an autumn picture!
- 🧲 Glue a magnet to the back for a fridge frame!
- 📅 Make seasonal frames — one for every holiday throughout the year!

## Why It's Amazing

* **Construction Skills:** Aligning sticks, applying glue, and building layered structures teaches basic construction and patience. 🏗️

* **Design Thinking:** Choosing colours, patterns, and decorations develops aesthetic sense and personal expression. 🎨

* **Gift-Giving:** Making a frame for someone else teaches children the value of thoughtful, handmade gifts. 🎁

* **Pride in Work:** Framing their own artwork shows children their creations are valued and worth displaying. ⭐

## Pro Tips

For **ages 3–5**: Pre-glue the frame and let them focus on painting and sticker decoration. Use chunky stickers and thick brushes. Help them choose a special drawing to frame.

For **ages 5–8**: Let them build the frame with guidance. Show them how overlapping corners creates strength. Challenge them to create a themed frame that matches the picture inside.

For **ages 8–12**: Introduce more complex designs — hexagonal frames, multi-opening frames, or frames with a "window" cut from card behind the sticks. Challenge them to make frames as gifts, thinking about what the recipient would like.

**Secret Pro Move**: Before painting, wrap the sticks with coloured washi tape for an instant no-mess decoration — the tape adds pattern and colour without any drying time! 🎀
