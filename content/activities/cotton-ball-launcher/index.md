## What You'll Create

Ready, aim, LAUNCH! 🚀 Your little engineers will build a brilliant cotton ball launcher from just a **paper cup** and a **balloon** — pull back, release, and watch the cotton ball fly! It's simple physics, massive fun, and once you've built one, you'll want to build a whole fleet. Set up targets around the room for a scoring game that will keep everyone entertained for ages!

## Have fun!

- 🏆 Create a tournament with elimination rounds!
- 📏 Measure how far each launch goes and try to break your record!
- 🎨 Decorate your launcher with stickers and markers — make it your signature weapon!
- 🤹 Try launching other soft items: scrunched paper balls, pom poms, mini marshmallows!

## Why It's Amazing

* **Physics in Action:** Children experience elastic potential energy, projectile motion, and force — all through play. Pulling back further = more energy = longer launch! 🔬

* **Engineering Thinking:** Building the launcher requires following construction steps and problem-solving when the balloon doesn't fit right. 🛠️

* **Measurement & Maths:** Keeping score, measuring distances, and comparing results introduces practical numeracy. 📊

* **Hand-Eye Coordination:** Aiming at targets and controlling the pull-back force develops fine motor control and spatial awareness. 🎯

## Pro Tips

For **ages 3–5**: Pre-build the launcher and let them focus on loading and launching. Use big cotton balls and large targets close by.

For **ages 5–8**: Let them help with assembly (adult cuts the cup). Introduce the concept of "fair test" — change one thing at a time (pull-back distance, ball size) and record results.

For **ages 8–12**: Challenge them to build multiple launchers with different cup sizes and compare performance. Can they design a launcher that's accurate rather than just powerful? Introduce angle — does launching upward vs. flat make a difference?

**Secret Pro Move**: Double-layer the balloon (stretch two cut balloons over the cup) for extra power — the cotton balls will fly twice as far! 💪
