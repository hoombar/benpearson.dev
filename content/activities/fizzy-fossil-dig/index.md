## What You'll Create

You're about to become a real-life palaeontologist! 🦖✨ First you'll mix up a magical fossil dough and bury tiny **dinosaur figurines** deep inside rock-shaped lumps. Then, once they've hardened, you'll use a special fizzy excavation spray — **white vinegar** — to watch your fossil rocks bubble, hiss, and crumble away, slowly revealing the prehistoric creatures trapped inside! It's science, sensory play, and dinosaur adventure all rolled into one.

## Have fun!

- 🔬 Talk about how REAL palaeontologists carefully dig fossils out of rock — yours fizz instead of chip!
- 🏷️ Give each fossil rock a name and guess which dinosaur is hiding inside before you excavate it.
- 🎨 Add different food colouring to each batch of dough — red for Mars rocks, blue for ocean fossils!
- ⏱️ Race to see who can uncover their dinosaur first using only the spray bottle.
- 📝 Keep a "dig journal" and draw each creature as you discover it.

## Why It's Amazing

* **Science Skills:** Children observe a real acid-base chemical reaction (vinegar + baking soda = carbon dioxide fizz!) happening right before their eyes. 🧪

* **Fine Motor Skills:** Packing the dough tightly, spraying the bottle, and carefully picking away softened rock all build hand strength and precision. ✋

* **Patience & Persistence:** Excavating takes time and care — kids learn to work methodically rather than rushing. 🐢

* **Curiosity & Imagination:** The hidden-toy surprise element sparks genuine excitement and curiosity about the natural world. 🌍

## Pro Tips

For **ages 3–5**: Pre-make the fossil rocks and focus the activity on the excavation. Use a dropper instead of a spray bottle for easier grip. Stick to 2–3 large rocks with big figurines that are easy to spot.

For **ages 5–8**: Let them mix the dough and bury the toys themselves. Encourage them to make their rocks different shapes — flat "slate" fossils, round "boulders", and egg shapes.

For **ages 8–12**: Challenge them to excavate as carefully as possible without touching the figurine with their fingers — just like a real dig! They can also experiment with the dough recipe (more or less water) and observe how it affects the fizzing speed.

💡 **Secret Trick**: Add a pinch of **glitter** to the dough before shaping — when the vinegar hits, your fossils will fizz AND sparkle like magic crystals! ✨
