## What You'll Create

You'll transform a plain recycling item into a fully functional musical instrument! 🎺 By stretching a tight membrane of **Baking Parchment** over a **Cardboard Tube**, you'll engineer a classic kazoo. When you hum into it, your voice vibrates the paper, creating a hilarious buzzing sound that's perfect for starting a family marching band!

## Have fun!

- 🥁 Start a family band with other homemade instruments like pots and wooden spoons.
- 🎵 Have a 'kazoo karaoke' contest where one person hums a song and the others try to guess it.
- 🗣️ Experiment by singing different vowel sounds (ah, ee, oo) to see how the buzz changes.
- 🕳️ Carefully poke a small hole in the side of the tube with a pencil and see how covering and uncovering it changes the pitch.

## Why It's Amazing

* **Science of Sound:** Demonstrates how sound travels through vibrations, letting kids literally feel the soundwaves! 📳
* **Musical Expression:** Encourages rhythm, melody recognition, and pure joyful noise-making. 🎵
* **Fine Motor Skills:** Stretching the paper and applying the rubber band requires coordination and grip strength. 🤏
* **Creative Upcycling:** Teaches that everyday rubbish can be transformed into something incredibly fun. ♻️

## Pro Tips

For **ages 3-5**: Pre-cut the parchment paper. They might find blowing easier than humming at first, so demonstrate how to 'sing' into the tube.

For **ages 5-8**: Let them try different materials for the membrane (e.g., tissue paper, tin foil, or cling film) to see which makes the best buzzing sound.

For **ages 8-12**: Challenge them to design a multi-tube kazoo or add keys to change the pitch.
