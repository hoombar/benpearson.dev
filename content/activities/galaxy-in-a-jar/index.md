## What You'll Create

You're about to become a cosmic creator! ✨ By layering soft **cotton wool**, sparkling **glitter**, and brilliant **food colouring**, you'll trap a miniature swirling galaxy inside a **glass jar**. It looks just like a real nebula deep in outer space! This isn't just a craft — it's a beautiful, calming sensory jar that you can keep on your windowsill to remind you of the stars. 🌌

## Have fun!

- 🌟 Place your jar in a sunny window and watch the stardust catch the light.
- 🚀 Use it as a prop for your next space-themed story or pretend mission to Mars.
- 🧘 Use it as a 'calm-down jar' — watching the swirling nebula can be very relaxing.

## Why It's Amazing

* **Sensory Engagement:** Exploring textures like fluffy cotton and liquid water creates a rich sensory experience. ✨
* **Colour Mixing:** Children observe how different liquid colours blend and transition within a solid medium. 🎨
* **Fine Motor Skills:** Stretching cotton wool and carefully pouring liquids develops hand control and precision. ✋
* **Patience and Focus:** Building the layers section by section encourages steady, mindful work. 🧠

## Pro Tips

For **ages 3–5**: They will love stretching the 'cloud' cotton wool! Help them with the pouring to avoid spills.

For **ages 5–8**: Encourage them to experiment with the amount of glitter — can they create a 'star-dense' region of space?

For **ages 8–12**: Challenge them to create a specific 'theme' — like a 'Fiery Supernova' with reds and oranges, or a 'Deep Sea Galaxy' with greens and teals.

**Secret Pro Move**: Use a **flashlight** to shine light through the bottom of the jar in the dark — your galaxy will truly come to life! 🔦
