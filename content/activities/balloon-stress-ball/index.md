## What You'll Create

The ultimate squeeze toy! 🎈 Your little scientists will fill a **balloon** with **plain flour** using a funnel to create a wonderfully squishy, squeezable stress ball. The flour-filled balloon moulds to your hand, bounces back into shape, and feels incredibly satisfying to squish. Draw a funny face with **markers** and you've got a pocket-sized fidget friend!

## Have fun!

- 😜 Draw different emotions — happy, sad, angry, surprised — for a feelings toolkit!
- 🎨 Layer two or three balloons of different colours with small holes cut in the outer ones for a polka-dot effect!
- 🤹 Make three and try juggling them — they're the perfect weight!
- 🔬 Try filling with rice, lentils, or sand — how does each filling feel different?

## Why It's Amazing

* **Sensory Science:** The flour-filled balloon demonstrates how a solid (flour) can behave like a liquid when contained — a concept called granular physics! 🔬

* **Emotional Regulation:** Squishing a stress ball is a proven technique for managing big feelings — kids learn a healthy coping strategy through play. 🧠

* **Fine Motor Strength:** Regular squeezing builds hand and finger muscle strength, which helps with handwriting and other precision tasks. ✋

* **Material Properties:** Comparing different fillings (flour vs. rice vs. sand) teaches observation, comparison, and descriptive vocabulary. 📊

## Pro Tips

For **ages 3–5**: Adults should handle the filling and tying. Let kids spoon flour into the funnel and do all the face drawing. The squishing is endlessly entertaining at this age!

For **ages 5–8**: Let them help with filling and tying. Make a set of emotion faces — happy, sad, angry, scared — and use them to talk about feelings. Great for emotional literacy!

For **ages 8–12**: Experiment with different fillings and record how each feels. Calculate how much flour fits in different balloon sizes. Research the science of granular materials — why does flour in a balloon feel solid when squeezed quickly but flow when moved slowly? (It's the same physics as quicksand!)

**Secret Pro Move**: Add a few drops of essential oil (lavender or peppermint) to the flour before filling — every squeeze releases a calming scent, making it a proper relaxation tool! 🎯
