Transform everyday cardboard tubes into an exhilarating marble roller coaster! This activity is a fantastic introduction to basic physics and structural engineering as you figure out the best angles and connections to keep your marble rolling.

## What You'll Create
An intricate pathway made of connected tubes where a marble can travel from top to bottom continuously.

## Have fun!
Start from the top and secure your first tube. Drop a marble through and see where it lands, then place the next tube there! Experiment with gaps, drops, and corners.

## Why It's Amazing
It's a wonderful combination of creative design, problem-solving, and physics in action. Adjusting the track to get the perfect run is deeply satisfying.

## Pro Tips
Use painter's tape if attaching to walls so you don't damage the paint! Cut some tubes in half lengthwise to create open 'half-pipe' sections for extra visibility.
