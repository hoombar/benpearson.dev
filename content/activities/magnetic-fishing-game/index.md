## What You'll Create

Get ready to cast a line! 🎣 You'll draw and colour a whole shoal of **paper** fish, clip a shiny **paper clip** to each one, then build a magnetic fishing rod from a **straw**, **string**, and a **magnet**. Spread the fish on the floor as your pond and take turns fishing — can you name the fish you catch? With numbers on each fish, it becomes a counting game too!

## How to Set It Up

### Step 1: Draw and Cut Your Fish

On a sheet of **paper**, use a **marker** to draw 8–12 fish shapes — each about the size of your palm. Make them different: some fat, some thin, some with spots, some with stripes. ⚠️ **Adult Helper Needed** — use **scissors** to cut them out, as the curves can be tricky for small hands. Colour each fish with **colouring pencils** and write a number on each one from 1 to 12.

### Step 2: Clip on the Paperclips

Pinch open a **paper clip** and thread the pointed end through the nose (front) of each fish, so the clip sticks out like a little beak. Squeeze it back flat against the paper. Test by holding the fish upside down — the clip should stay on firmly. Repeat for every fish.

### Step 3: Build Your Fishing Rod

Take a **straw** and cut a piece of **string** about 50 cm long. Tie one end of the string securely to the tip of the straw with a double knot — wrap it around twice and pull tight. Then tie the other end of the string to a small **magnet** with another double knot. Tug the string gently to test — the magnet should dangle freely below the rod tip.

### Step 4: Set Up the Pond

Spread all the fish face-up on the floor in a rough circle — that's your pond! Stand back about an arm's length and lower the magnet toward a fish's paper clip. When the magnet snaps onto the clip, slowly lift the rod — you've made a catch! 🐟 Take turns, count how many each player catches, and add up the numbers on the fish for a bonus score.

## Have fun!

- 🔢 Add the numbers on all your caught fish together — whose total score is highest?
- 🏅 Give different fish different point values (big fish = 5 points, small fish = 1 point).
- ⏱️ Set a 30-second timer and see how many fish you can catch in one go.
- 🦈 Draw a shark card — if you catch it, you lose a point!

## Why It's Amazing

* **Counting and Maths:** Adding up the numbers on caught fish builds number fluency in a genuinely fun context. 🔢
* **Fine Motor Skills:** Guiding the magnet to the paper clip develops hand-eye coordination and precision. 🎯
* **Science Concepts:** Children experience magnetism first-hand — discovering that magnets attract metal but not paper. 🧲
* **Social Skills:** Taking turns, counting scores, and following game rules builds patience and fair play. 🤝

## Pro Tips

For **ages 4–5**: Focus on catching and counting — skip the scoring. Use big fish for easier targets.

For **ages 5–7**: Add the numbers on the fish they catch for a running total. Challenge them to reach 20 before switching turns.

**Tip**: Use a stronger magnet (a fridge magnet works well) if the paper clips are slipping off. Sticky-taping the paper clip flat to each fish also helps them stay attached during lifting.
