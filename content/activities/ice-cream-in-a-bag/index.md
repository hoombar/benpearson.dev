## What You'll Create

You are about to become an arctic food scientist! 🍨 By mixing freezing ice with special salt, you'll create an environment so cold that it instantly freezes liquid cream into a delicious, sweet dessert right before your eyes.

## Have fun!

- 🎵 Invent an 'Ice Cream Shaking Dance' to do while you toss the bag back and forth.
- 🍓 Add chocolate chips, strawberry syrup, or crushed cookies to the cream bag before freezing.
- ⏱️ Use a stopwatch to see exactly how many seconds it takes to go from liquid to solid.

## Why It's Amazing

* **Endothermic Reactions:** The salt lowers the freezing point of water, drawing heat away from the cream. It's real, edible chemistry! 🧪
* **Sensory Play:** The drastic temperature change and physical shaking provide brilliant tactile feedback. ❄️
* **Instant Reward:** Seeing a physical state change (liquid to solid) happen so fast makes science tangible and tasty.

## Pro Tips

⚠️ **Adult Helper Needed** — The ice/salt mixture can reach temperatures below -10°C! It is crucial to wear winter gloves or wrap the bag in a thick towel while shaking to prevent cold burns on little hands.
