## What You'll Create

You and your child will become master course designers, transforming your living room floor into an epic, 4-hole mini-golf tournament! You will craft cardboard tunnels, ramps, and trick-shots, using a **cardboard tube** as your putter and **marbles** as your golf balls.

## Have fun!

- 🏆 Keep a scorecard and crown the Mini-Golf Champion of the house!
- 🎨 Decorate each hole with a different theme using markers or paint.
- 🔄 Rearrange the obstacles to make a brand-new course tomorrow.
- 🎶 Play some upbeat stadium music in the background.

## Why It's Amazing

* **Gross Motor Skills:** Swinging the club with just the right amount of force teaches coordination. 🏌️
* **Engineering Thinking:** Designing the course requires spatial awareness and testing. 🏗️
* **Math Skills:** Keeping score is a fun, sneaky way to practice addition! ➕
* **Family Bonding:** A little friendly competition is great for everyone. ❤️

## Pro Tips

For **ages 3-5**: Skip the club and let them roll the marble with their hands! Make the archways very wide.

For **ages 5-8**: Let them design the course. Challenge them to include at least one ramp or bridge.

For **ages 8-12**: Add 'par' scores to each hole. Introduce trick shots, like bouncing off a wall to make it in.
