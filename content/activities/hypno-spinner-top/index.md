## What You'll Create

Prepare to be HYPNOTISED! 🌀✨ Your little physicists will craft a spinning top from just a circle of card and a **toothpick**, then decorate it with bold colour patterns that transform into mind-bending optical illusions when it spins! Red and blue stripes blur into purple. A rainbow spiral becomes a shimmering silver disc. It's science you can see — and it never gets old!

## Have fun!

- 🌀 Make several spinners with different patterns and compare the optical effects!
- ⏱️ Have a "longest spin" competition — whose top keeps going the longest?
- 🔬 Predict what colour each pattern will become BEFORE spinning — test your hypothesis!
- 🎨 Try a black-and-white spiral pattern — does it appear to move even after it stops? (It does!)

## Why It's Amazing

* **Colour Theory:** Children discover colour mixing through physics — seeing red + blue = purple happen before their eyes through motion, not paint! 🎨

* **Optical Science:** The spinning disc demonstrates persistence of vision — the same principle behind movies and TV screens! 🔬

* **Physics of Rotation:** Finding the exact centre, balancing the weight, and experimenting with spin speed teaches rotational physics intuitively. ⚖️

* **Prediction & Testing:** Guessing what colours will appear and then testing builds the scientific method naturally. 🧪

## Pro Tips

For **ages 3–5**: Pre-cut the circle and poke the hole. Let them focus on colouring bold, simple patterns (half-and-half works best). Help them spin it.

For **ages 5–8**: Let them cut and assemble independently. Challenge them to try 3 different patterns and rank which creates the best illusion.

For **ages 8–12**: Introduce the science vocabulary (persistence of vision, additive colour mixing). Challenge them to create a spinner that makes a completely NEW colour appear that isn't on the disc at all.

**Secret Pro Move**: Draw a thin black spiral line on a white disc. Spin it for 20 seconds, then look at your hand — your hand appears to BREATHE and move! This is the motion aftereffect illusion and it's absolutely wild. 🤯
