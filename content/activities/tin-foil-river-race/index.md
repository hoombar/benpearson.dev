## What You'll Create

Turn your garden or patio into a wild river rapids course! 🌊 Using long sheets of **Aluminium Foil**, you will engineer a custom riverbed. Once you add **Water**, you'll have a flowing stream perfect for racing a **Small Plastic Figurine** or a bottle cap. It's a fantastic way to learn about gravity and water flow while having a splash!

## Have fun!

- 🏁 Race two different toys at once and see which shape is more aerodynamic.
- 🪵 Add 'natural' obstacles like twigs or leaves to see how they affect the flow.
- 🏗️ Try to build a 'dam' out of stones and see if your river can overflow it.
- ⏱️ Use a stopwatch to time how long it takes for a toy to travel from start to finish.

## Why It's Amazing

* **Physics and Engineering:** Children learn about gravity, slopes, and how water takes the path of least resistance. 🧪
* **Problem Solving:** If the water leaks or stops, kids have to figure out how to fix the 'levee' or increase the slope. 🏗️
* **Sensory Play:** Engaging with water on a sunny day is a great tactile and cooling experience. ☀️
* **Creativity:** Designing the course layout allows for endless variations and fun. 🎨

## Pro Tips

For **ages 3-5**: Keep the river short (1-2 metres) and help them fold the walls. Focus on the simple joy of pouring and watching things move.

For **ages 5-8**: Encourage them to build 'locks' or 'dams' to control the water flow.

For **ages 8-12**: Challenge them to build a river that changes direction at least three times without leaking!
