## What You'll Create

Your own designer eyewear! 👓 Your little fashion designers will bend and twist colourful **pipe cleaners** into wearable spectacles with lenses in any shape they choose — classic circles, funky hearts, cool stars, or dramatic cat-eyes. Add extra pipe cleaners for decorations and colour combinations. The perfect accessory for dress-up, photo booths, or silly selfies!

## Have fun!

- 🕶️ Make different styles — round hippy glasses, heart-shaped party specs, star-shaped rock star shades!
- 👽 Add antennae on top for alien glasses or spirals for crazy scientist specs!
- 📸 Set up a photo booth with all your different glasses and take silly photos!
- 🎭 Create character glasses — Harry Potter rounds, Elton John stars, superhero masks!

## Why It's Amazing

* **Fine Motor Mastery:** Bending, twisting, and wrapping pipe cleaners builds exceptional finger strength and dexterity. ✋

* **Shape Geometry:** Creating consistent circles, hearts, and stars from a straight line teaches how shapes are constructed from curves and angles. 📐

* **Symmetry Skills:** Making both lenses match requires careful observation and bilateral symmetry — a key mathematical concept. 🔬

* **Self-Expression:** Designing their own wearable accessory encourages personal style, creativity, and confidence. 🌟

## Pro Tips

For **ages 3–5**: Start with simple circles — wrap each pipe cleaner around a toilet roll tube to get a perfect round shape. Help with twisting and connecting. They'll love wearing the finished glasses!

For **ages 5–8**: Challenge them to make matching heart or star lenses. Show them how to wrap contrasting colours around the frames. Make a pair for every family member.

For **ages 8–12**: Design elaborate frames — cat-eye shapes, aviator styles, or fantasy designs. Create a "glasses shop" with different styles displayed on cardboard stands. Add decorative elements like pipe cleaner flowers on the corners. Try making 3D frames that extend outward!

**Secret Pro Move**: Before shaping, straighten your pipe cleaners by running them through your closed fist a few times — this removes kinks and gives you much smoother, more professional-looking curves! 🎯
