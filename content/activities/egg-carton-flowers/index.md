## What You'll Create

A bouquet that lasts forever! 🌸 Your little gardeners will cut individual cups from an **egg carton**, snip the edges into petal shapes, paint them in gorgeous colours with **poster paint**, and thread **pipe cleaner** stems through the centre. The result? A stunning bouquet of flowers that will brighten any room and never need watering. Perfect for gifts, decorations, or just because!

## Have fun!

- 🌈 Make a rainbow bouquet — one flower in each colour of the rainbow!
- 💝 Create a Mother's Day or birthday bouquet as a handmade gift!
- 🐝 Add a pipe cleaner bee or butterfly perching on one of the flowers!
- 🏡 Make a whole garden scene by standing flowers in a tray of playdough!

## Why It's Amazing

* **Upcycling Creativity:** Transforming rubbish (egg cartons) into beautiful art teaches children that creative materials are everywhere — no craft shop needed! ♻️

* **Scissor Skills:** Cutting petal shapes from the thick cardboard develops hand strength and precision cutting ability. ✂️

* **Colour and Design:** Choosing colours, planning petal patterns, and arranging a bouquet exercises artistic decision-making and aesthetic sense. 🎨

* **3D Construction:** Building a flower from flat materials into a standing 3D object develops spatial thinking and engineering skills. 🏗️

## Pro Tips

For **ages 3–5**: An adult should cut the cups and shape the petals. Let the little ones focus on painting (their favourite part!) and choosing colours. Use thicker pipe cleaners that are easier for small hands to push through holes. Pre-poke the stem holes.

For **ages 5–8**: They can cut and shape their own petals with supervision. Encourage experimenting with different petal styles. Talk about real flowers — how many petals does a daisy have? A tulip? Try to make flowers that look like real ones!

For **ages 8–12**: Challenge them to create botanically inspired flowers — research real flower structures and replicate them. Make centres from scrunched tissue paper, add stamens from thin pipe cleaner pieces, and create realistic leaves with painted veins. A truly impressive display!

**Secret Pro Move**: Before painting, give each flower a coat of white PVA glue mixed with a tiny bit of water — this seals the cardboard and makes the paint colours much more vibrant. Also, spray the finished flowers with clear hairspray to seal the paint and add a lovely sheen! ✨
