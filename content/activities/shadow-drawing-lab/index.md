## What You'll Create

You're about to turn light and shadows into a masterpiece! 🌟 In the **Shadow Drawing Lab**, you'll learn how to position **Small Plastic Figurines** so they cast long, cool shadows onto a sheet of **Paper**. By tracing these shapes with **Markers**, you can capture silhouettes that look like they've jumped straight out of a storybook!

## Have fun!

- 🦖 Create a prehistoric scene by tracing multiple dinosaur shadows.
- 🌈 Colour in your silhouettes with bright patterns or wild colours.
- 🕰️ Try tracing at different times of day to see how the sun changes the shadow's length.
- 🔦 Use different coloured light sources for a 'disco shadow' effect.

## Why It's Amazing

* **Artistic Observation:** Children learn to look closely at shapes and outlines rather than just details. 🎨
* **Science of Light:** It's a hands-on lesson in how light travels and how shadows are formed. 💡
* **Fine Motor Skills:** Tracing complex silhouettes requires focus and precise hand-eye coordination. ✍️
* **Patience & Focus:** Staying steady while tracing builds concentration skills. 🧘

## Pro Tips

For **ages 3–5**: Use simpler toys like blocks or large chunky animals with clear, bold outlines.

For **ages 5–8**: Challenge them to create a story by overlapping different shadows to make new 'hybrid' monsters.

For **ages 8–12**: Introduce the concept of 'forced perspective' — moving the light source to make a tiny toy look like a giant monster on the page.
