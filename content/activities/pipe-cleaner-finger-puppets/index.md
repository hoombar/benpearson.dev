## What You'll Create

Lights, camera, fingers! 🎭 Your little puppet masters will twist and bend colourful **pipe cleaners** into adorable tiny characters that perch right on their fingertips. Make a whole cast — animals, people, monsters, aliens — add **googly eyes** for instant personality, and put on the most entertaining finger puppet show your living room has ever seen!

## Have fun!

- 🐾 Make a set of farm animals — cow, pig, chicken, horse, and sheep!
- 👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 Create finger puppet versions of your whole family!
- 📖 Retell a favourite story — Three Little Pigs works brilliantly with finger puppets!
- 🌍 Make puppets from different countries and learn about world cultures!

## Why It's Amazing

* **Fine Motor Mastery:** Twisting, bending, and shaping pipe cleaners around tiny fingers develops exceptional dexterity and hand strength. ✋

* **Storytelling and Language:** Creating characters and performing stories develops narrative skills, vocabulary, and confidence in speaking. 📚

* **Creative Problem-Solving:** Working out how to make a 3D character from bendy sticks requires real engineering thinking and spatial awareness. 🧠

* **Social-Emotional Skills:** Giving puppets personalities and emotions helps children explore and express feelings through safe, imaginative play. 💫

## Pro Tips

For **ages 3–5**: An adult should make the finger loop (small fingers can be fiddly). Keep characters simple — just ears and eyes make a perfectly good bunny! Focus on the imaginative play rather than complex construction.

For **ages 5–8**: They can make their own finger loops with a bit of practice. Encourage them to plan their character before building — "What does a dragon need? Wings? A tail? Horns?" Challenge them to create a cast and write a short story.

For **ages 8–12**: Challenge them to create articulated puppets with movable parts — jointed arms, opening mouths using clever folding. Make a proper puppet theatre from a cardboard box. Write scripts, design sets, and perform for family!

**Secret Pro Move**: Wrap the finger loop section with a second pipe cleaner in a contrasting colour — it makes the puppet sit more securely on the finger AND looks more polished. For tiny facial features, use a fine-point permanent marker directly on the pipe cleaner surface! ✍️
